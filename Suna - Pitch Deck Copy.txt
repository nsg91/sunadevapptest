3.3  SCANNING  (60-second countdown, BPM + SI emerge)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Rotating wisdom — sensor:
  • Your camera is reading 30 frames a second of light through
    your fingertip.
  • The flash is the same wavelength used in hospital pulse
    oximeters.
  • Each beat moves the blood in your finger. The sensor sees
    every one.
  • PPG — photoplethysmography. The same tech in your Oura ring
    is in your phone.
  • Your finger is bright red right now. That's how the sensor
    knows it's a finger.
  • 60 seconds is enough for the Baevsky Index. Astronauts used
    the same math in orbit.

Rotating wisdom — breath:
  • Breathe like normal. The sensor is calibrating to you.
  • Soften your jaw. Drop your shoulders. The data will show it.
  • Lengthen the exhale. Twice as long as the inhale, if you can.
  • Unclench your hand. Your fingertip should feel warm, not
    pressed.
  • Slow it down. The sensor reads slowness as truth.
  • Notice your feet on the floor.

Closer (last 15s — one of):
  • One last breath. Your system is being heard.
  • Almost there. Your nervous system has been seen.
  • Reading you now. Hold steady.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3.4  ERROR STATES  (~10% chance after 3s, never on retry)
----------------------------------------------------------------
NO FINGER:
  I can't see your finger.
  Cover both the lens and the flash with your fingertip.
  [Try again]   [I'll come back]

LOW LIGHT:
  Not enough light.
  Move to a brighter spot, or hold the flash closer to your
  fingertip.

NOISY / MOTION:
  Too much motion.
  Rest your hand on a flat surface and breathe steady.

LOST SIGNAL:
  I lost your pulse.
  Soften your finger — you may be pressing too hard.

✓ COMPLETE

══ RISE / Sparks  (morning, 6–11a) ══

1. SACCADIC RESET
   Subtitle:    Lateral eye movement
   Description: Triggers optic flow, suppressing the amygdala
                and releasing the behavioral brake.
   Performance: Negotiation: 20% more likely to initiate
                high-stakes asks.
   The Data:    Triggers Optic Flow, suppressing the Amygdala.
   Source:      Huberman Lab / Stanford (2021)
   Steps:       Fix gaze on a point ahead
                Move eyes smoothly left to right
                Follow with gentle head turns
                Let the horizon expand

2. AURICULAR RESET
   Subtitle:    Vagal ear activation
   Description: Stimulates the concha — the only place the
                vagus nerve reaches the skin surface.
   Performance: Improves gut motility. Increases blood flow
                for a natural morning glow.
   The Data:    Stimulates the Concha for vagal activation.
   Source:      He, W., et al. (2012)
   Steps:       Gently pull outer ear upward, hold 5s
                Pull outward, hold 5s
                Pull earlobe down, hold 5s
                Circular pressure on the tragus

3. SOMATIC SHAKE
   Subtitle:    Micro-movement discharge
   Description: Resets muscle spindle fibers, discharging
                trapped sympathetic energy.
   Performance: Discharges pre-meeting adrenaline. Clears
                sleep stiffness.
   The Data:    Resets Muscle Spindle Fibers.
   Source:      Levine, P.A. (1997)
   Steps:       Stand with feet hip-width apart
                Begin shaking hands gently
                Let the shake travel through arms and
                  shoulders
                Allow micro-tremors to release naturally

4. COLD POINT
   Subtitle:    Mammalian dive reflex
   Description: Triggers the mammalian dive reflex, spiking
                norepinephrine for alertness.
   Performance: 3 hours of sharp focus. Depuffs and tightens
                pores.
   The Data:    Spikes Norepinephrine via Mammalian Dive
                Reflex.
   Source:      Alboni, P., et al. (2011)
   Steps:       Apply cold water or cold object to face
                Focus on the temples and forehead
                Breathe slowly through the sensation
                Notice the alertness arriving

══ FLOW  (midday, 11a–4p) ══

5. 0.1Hz WAVE
   Subtitle:    6 breaths per minute
   Description: Creates autonomic coherence at 6 breaths per
                minute (0.1 Hz).
   Performance: Peak emotional control. 15% less biological
                effort to work.
   The Data:    Creates Autonomic Coherence at 0.1 Hz.
   Source:      Lehrer, P.M., et al. (2003)
   Steps:       Inhale for 5 seconds
                Exhale for 5 seconds
                Find the wave rhythm
                Let coherence build naturally

6. SOFT GAZE
   Subtitle:    Panoramic vision shift
   Description: Shifts from focal to panoramic vision,
                instantly disengaging high-alert.
   Performance: See the big-picture strategy. Prevents brow
                furrows.
   The Data:    Shifts from Focal to Panoramic Vision.
   Source:      Vision Research Journal
   Steps:       Soften your gaze — let focus blur
                Expand awareness to peripheral vision
                Notice space above and below sight line
                Hold the panoramic field for 30s

7. BUTTERFLY HUG
   Subtitle:    Bilateral stimulation
   Description: Uses bilateral stimulation to integrate logic
                and creative brain hemispheres.
   Performance: Breakthrough problem-solving. Mops up mid-day
                stress.
   The Data:    Bilateral Stimulation integrates left/right
                brain.
   Source:      Artigas, L. (1998) / EMDR
   Steps:       Cross arms over chest, hands on shoulders
                Alternate tapping left and right
                Keep rhythm slow and steady
                Close eyes and let the mind settle

8. HEART-CENTERED HOLD
   Subtitle:    Oxytocin activation
   Description: Triggers oxytocin release, a biological buffer
                against mid-day cortisol.
   Performance: Presence feels magnetic and trustworthy. Evens
                complexion.
   The Data:    Triggers Oxytocin as a Cortisol buffer.
   Source:      Uvnäs-Moberg, K. (2003)
   Steps:       Place both hands over your heart
                Feel the warmth build beneath your palms
                Breathe slowly into the contact point
                Notice the settling that follows

══ LAND / Anchors  (evening, 4p–12a) ══

9. PHYSIOLOGICAL SIGH
   Subtitle:    Double inhale · long exhale
   Description: Pops the alveoli to maximize CO₂ offload and
                lower heart rate.
   Performance: The emergency exit for a bad day. Relaxes jaw
                and neck.
   The Data:    Pops Alveoli for maximum CO₂ offload.
   Source:      Vlemincx, E., et al. (2010)
   Steps:       Double inhale through the nose — two sniffs
                One long exhale through the mouth
                Let the second sniff re-inflate the lungs
                Repeat 3–5 cycles

10. BIRD SONG
    Subtitle:    Listen, with your eyes closed
    Description: Birdsong sits in a frequency band that
                 promotes alpha brain waves and signals safety
                 to the nervous system.
    Performance: Lowers cortisol within minutes. The body reads
                 it as: no predators here.
    The Data:    Activates Alpha Waves via 1–8 kHz acoustic
                 range.
    Source:      Stuhlmann et al. (2022) · Ratcliffe et al.
                 (2013)
    Steps:       Close your eyes
                 Let the sound fill the room
                 Notice nearer and farther voices
                 That's the practice

11. FRONTAL-OCCIPITAL BRIDGE
    Subtitle:    Prefrontal blood flow
    Description: Forces blood flow from the survival brain
                 back to the prefrontal cortex.
    Performance: Induces deep sleep cycles. Natural botox for
                 the forehead.
    The Data:    Redirects blood to Prefrontal Cortex.
    Source:      Siegel, D.J. (2012)
    Steps:       Place one hand on forehead
                 Place other hand on back of head
                 Hold gently with light pressure
                 Breathe and feel the warmth equalize

12. VAGAL HUM
    Subtitle:    Nitric oxide resonance
    Description: Increases nitric oxide by 15x, dilating
                 vessels for deep recovery.
    Performance: Clears vocal fatigue. Vibrational lymphatic
                 drainage.
    The Data:    Increases Nitric Oxide 15x via humming
                 resonance.
    Source:      Weitzberg & Lundberg (2002)
    Steps:       Inhale deeply through the nose
                 Hum on the exhale at a low pitch
                 Feel vibration travel throat to chest
                 Let each hum deepen naturally

══ PLAY  (unmeasured · anytime) ══

13. THE CURIOSITY SCAN
    Subtitle:    Five things you never noticed
    Description: Find five things in the room you've never
                 noticed before. Not things you haven't looked
                 at — things that weren't there, to you, until
                 now.
    Performance: Norepinephrine release without the alarm.
                 Hippocampal encoding ramps up — time slows.
    The Data:    Activates the Locus Coeruleus via novelty
                 detection.
    Source:      Panksepp (1998) · Eichenbaum (2017) ·
                 Aston-Jones & Cohen (2005)
    Steps:       Look around the room
                 Find one thing you've never noticed
                 Then another. And another.
                 Stop when you find the fifth

14. THE FOUND OBJECT
    Subtitle:    Use it as something else
    Description: Pick up something within arm's reach. Use it
                 as something else. A spoon becomes a
                 microphone. A phone becomes a bird.
    Performance: Engages the default mode network — where
                 spontaneous insight lives.
    The Data:    Recruits the DMN, quiets the central
                 executive.
    Source:      Beaty (2016) · Christoff et al. (2016)
    Steps:       Pick up something within arm's reach
                 Make it into something else
                 Speak to it. Use it. Play with it.
                 Stop when you surprise yourself

15. DANCE LIKE NO ONE'S WATCHING
    Subtitle:    One song. No plan.
    Description: Put on one song. Move however you want. No
                 mirror. No phone. No plan.
    Performance: 20 minutes of elevated divergent thinking
                 afterward. Cortisol drops measurably.
    The Data:    Recruits motor cortex + cerebellum + limbic
                 simultaneously.
    Source:      Koelsch (2014) · Karageorghis & Priest (2012)
                 · Lewis & Lovatt (2013)
    Steps:       Pick one song, don't overthink it
                 Move however you want
                 No mirror. No phone. No plan.
                 Stop when the song stops

11 — MONEY & PERFORMANCE FACTS  (shuffleable card on Home)
================================================================

01. THE NEGOTIATION GAP
    Headline:  Anxious negotiators walk away with 30% less.
    Pull:      the fastest ROI in your workday.
    Body:      Wharton: anxious participants made lower first
               offers, accepted worse counters, exited earlier.
               90 seconds of physiological sighing flips the
               profile.
    Source:    Brooks & Schweitzer, 2011

02. THE TRADER DATA
    Headline:  High-HRV traders outperform over months of
               real P&L.
    Pull:      your heart rhythm is a market edge.
    Body:      MIT tracked working traders. HRV predicted
               risk-adjusted returns better than years of
               experience. Regulation compounds.
    Source:    Lo & Repin, 2002

03. THE RAISE
    Headline:  Vocal prosody predicts compensation.
    Pull:      the voice in the room is the number on the
               contract.
    Body:      Speakers with flat or unregulated tone get
               lower offers — even when the words are
               identical. Hum before the call.
    Source:    Porges, 2011

04. THE ASK
    Headline:  20% more likely to initiate a high-stakes ask
               after a Saccadic Reset.
    Pull:      the most valuable 60 seconds of your morning.
    Body:      Optic flow reduces amygdala activation and
               increases willingness to initiate. The practice
               most directly tied to income.
    Source:    Huberman Lab, 2021

05. THE CORTISOL TAX
    Headline:  Every 10% rise in cortisol drops risk-taking.
    Pull:      the hormone that talks you out of the deal.
    Body:      Coates and Herbert studied London traders in
               live markets. High-cortisol days produced
               conservative calls. A physiological sigh drops
               cortisol within minutes.
    Source:    Coates & Herbert, 2008

06. THE RECOVERY GAP
    Headline:  Top performers come back faster after a no.
    Pull:      not whether it happens. how fast you recover.
    Body:      Top-percentile athletes, traders, and
               negotiators show shorter HRV-drop durations
               after a loss. Recovery speed is trainable.
    Source:    Thayer & Lane, 2000

13 — STATE-FRAMEWORK (reference, used throughout)
================================================================

THE BAEVSKY STRESS INDEX (SI) maps to four states:
  SI > 150   →  BRITTLE   (red-lining, brace)
  SI 100–150 →  REACTIVE  (alert, scattered)
  SI 50–100  →  ELASTIC   (sweet spot, bounce)
  SI < 50    →  STATIC    (quiet, low momentum)

THE FOUR PHASES OF THE DAY:
  rise   (6a–11a)   — clear sleep inertia, build momentum
  flow   (11a–4p)   — lock coherence, widen the window
  land   (4p–12a)   — deep parasympathetic, recover
  play   (anytime)  — unmeasured, by design uncounted

CYCLE PHASES (when tracking on):
  Days 1–5         menstrual
  Days 6–~12       follicular
  Days ~13–~15     ovulation
  Days ~16–~23     luteal
  Days ~24–end     late luteal
